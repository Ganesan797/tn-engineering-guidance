## Threshold evaluation and next work

All proposed thresholds remain PROPOSED_OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED. 116/116 dispositions: KEEP as accountability, with human review and held codes preserved; reducing risks omissions, expanding scope costs review. ≥50% reviewed profiles: 38/84 and 73/84 are **potential**, not reviewed; CANNOT_JUDGE_YET, with considerable mapping/copy effort and no pressure to clear holds. ≥20 multi-college profiles: 20 and 32 potential, not reviewed; CANNOT_JUDGE_YET; low breadth weakens choice, high targets penalize rare branches. All six families: both have candidates, zero release-ready; KEEP as a proposed representation goal where supported, with hold exceptions. Ten districts: eight non-conflicting sample labels plus unknowns, so CANNOT_JUDGE_YET; lower may narrow geography, higher increases reconciliation/maintenance. ≥80% reviewed pair dispositions with 100% accounted exact pairs: selected pairs 169/301 are accounted structurally, governance review pending; KEEP accounting, CANNOT_JUDGE_YET for 80%. Lower risks omissions; higher costs review and may delay launch. Source conflicts cannot be resolved automatically.

Proposed initial journey: use reviewed subset of cohort only, branch profile → source-year options → context → neutral comparison → checks; college → source-year programmes → reviewed profile link → compare → checks. Held/unapproved profiles can be shown as unlinked or pending only under the accepted contract, never presented as approved; rare branches and statewide metadata can remain deferred. Profile/copy approval is a precondition to displaying explanations. An exact code match is not an approval.

Automatable: extraction, exact-code joins, checks, version comparison and source-page recording. Humans: source conflict reconciliation, exact programme/profile identity decisions, approval of bilingual wording and college metadata. Planning estimate, not measured: 1–3 engineer-days for repeatable source/parser QA and bounded cohort preparation; 2–6 reviewer-days for 15 colleges/169 pairs and selected profile/copy decisions, with 25/301 roughly 4–10 reviewer-days. Ranges are not validated time studies; 26 holds in the preferred set may expand them materially. Annual updates need fresh source snapshots, incremental diff/reconciliation and renewed source/copy review. Before release the owner must decide actual coverage/thresholds and separately authorize a bounded governance task.
