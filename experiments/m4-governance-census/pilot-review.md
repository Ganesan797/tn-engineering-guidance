2. **Nine direct code links:** review the seven existing profile identities and nine direct rows against the ledger and relevant source scope; decide each proposed link independently. Preserve the two variants and AE hold until their own review.

4. **Seven content profiles:** factual page/source-scope check, English review and human Tamil equivalence/usability review; consider the corrections above. A04 reuse is limited to its earlier approved scope.
5. **Coverage and first slice:** choose a boundary and approve or revise proposed thresholds; decide how pending/held identities are displayed and which source-year context is useful.

Proposed first useful slice, **OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED**: use this seven-college/12-pair research cohort as the target upper bound for the first governed implementation, subject to resolving or safely excluding individual unsupported records. Aim for 5–7 colleges, 8–12 source-backed pairs and at least 4–6 human-reviewed profiles, with two or more options for at least one approved profile and at least three supported families. The cohort has four families and four non-conflicting booklet district labels (Chennai, Chengalpattu, Krishnagiri, Nagapattinam); 1303 and 4967 remain unknown due to disputed districts. Support at least three verified locations and the university/government/constituent/self-financing contexts where verified. Required: code, official name, exact programme code/name, dated source/page, reviewed profile association, and independent management/autonomy/minority fields when evidenced. Unknown nonessential attributes may remain unknown; identity and offering evidence for displayed rows are mandatory. The owner must decide whether this is sufficiently useful given the limited depth and held content.

Full M4 completion still requires comprehensive verified in-scope educational branch coverage with every existing code/profile accounted for, all holds and scope decisions made or explicitly disposed, and required factual/Tamil/English reviews. Rare unresolved profiles, statewide college-attribute completeness, fee/hostel/accreditation/placement details and export may be deferred from the first slice. The first slice must not silently hide the rest of the catalogue or claim statewide completeness.
