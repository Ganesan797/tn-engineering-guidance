## Precise unresolved evidence limits

1. **Q021 numeric cutoff:** No college, admission year, academic/vocational stream, counselling round/type, community/allotted category, quota/special reservation or programme/seat type was supplied. Producing one number would mix incomparable records. The official lookup route is supplied instead.
2. **Q045–Q046 current hiring:** No single current employer requirement represents the whole labour market. The draft therefore gives occupation/programme pathways and tells the student to verify the target employer’s current eligibility. It does not claim typical placement or branch-neutral hiring.
3. **Autonomous institutions:** The branch comparisons use Anna University’s non-autonomous affiliated-institution R2025 Revised 1 curricula. An autonomous college must be checked against its own syllabus.
4. **Tamil equivalence:** Tamil is editorial draft and still needs native-speaker/volunteer review for naturalness and complete semantic equivalence. English completeness does not approve Tamil.
5. **Actual student comprehension:** The paper walkthrough is a review prediction. A real zero-knowledge student has not validated comprehension or the 5–10-minute value goal.
6. **Cutoff portal availability:** `https://cutoff.tneaonline.org/` returned HTTP 503 during the final link check on 2026-10-02. Its indexed official description and 2021–2025 scope were available during research, but a student may need to retry the official portal later. No alternate unofficial cutoff source or numeric value is substituted.

## Community observations kept separate

C01 and C02 show that student questions often combine course labels with cutoff, placement, fees, location and campus experience. That observation supports preserving the student’s named entities and separating curriculum fit from admission feasibility. The posts and replies are not used for any factual statement, recommendation, cutoff, college claim or outcome in V2.
