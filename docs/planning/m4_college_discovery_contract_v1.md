## Student outcome and boundaries

Can a student without marks or admissions knowledge explore the existing engineering programme catalogue, find colleges listed with connected programmes in official evidence, understand institution context, and know what to check next? M4 is marks-free, Tamil-first Explore / Think Further. Use existing reviewed material and keep Tamil/English accessible. General browsing, show all and not sure require no profile or inferred preference. A source-year listing is not proof of current participation, seats, eligibility or admission.

Current direction also excludes historical cutoff/rank/community matching from M4. ## Existing catalogue and source authority

Reuse the existing six families C, E, M, B, T and S, the 84 proposed profiles and the complete 116-code 2026 post-Round-1 census, including six separately held scope codes. The review records 53 curriculum-anchored drafts and 31 evidence/identity holds; none of the 84 proposed profiles has final student-display approval. The eight earlier A04 explanations are reusable only within their actual review and evidence scope. A curriculum anchor describes an institution/regulation's education and does not establish a college offering, equivalence, current availability or student suitability. Do not create new profiles, merge near names, discard rare codes, or resolve identity/scope holds by similarity.

Authority depends on the claim:

- **Programme offering:** official TNEA/DoTE seat matrix → governed repository derivative → secondary directory. The matrix provides the printed college code/name and official programme/branch code/name, and proves that the pair appears in the 2026 General Academic stream at the stated pre-special-reservation counselling stage. Category-wise counts apply only to that scope and may be used later only with separate governance. The listing does not establish every programme outside that scope, later-round participation, permanent or future offering, current vacancy, historical cutoff, quality, suitability or admission likelihood.
- **College details:** use the applicable official source for the specific field and date, including [2026 Information About Colleges](https://static.tneaonline.org/docs/Information_about_colleges_2026.pdf?t=1793121853360) where the relevant field is actually stated, followed by a governed derivative and secondary directory. For example, the booklet may support a published address; autonomy needs an explicit contemporaneous source. The booklet was downloaded for a bounded inspection described below; statewide completeness is not established. Verify each field and page before ingestion; an unverified field is UNKNOWN. The existing five abbreviated names are governed pilot labels, not a verified full-name inventory.
- **Educational branch meaning:** reviewed and governed content from the existing M4 branch catalogue, grounded in approved curriculum evidence. The current catalogue is a research draft; this authority order does not approve any candidate text, mapping or curriculum claim for display.

For source-year college/programme offerings use [DoTE/TNEA 2026 General Academic seat matrix before special reservation counselling](https://static.tneaonline.org/docs/GENERAL_ACADEMIC_SEAT_MATRIX_BEFORE_SPECIAL_RESERVATION_COUNSELLING_2026.pdf), which lists college code/name and branch code/name, and is the direct document corresponding to repository `SRC005`. Its 2026 snapshot is not evidence of present-day availability. The [2026 General Academic vacancy position after Round 1](https://static.tneaonline.org/docs/TNEA-2026-GENERAL-ACADEMIC-VACANCY-POSITION-AFTER-ROUND-1.pdf) is the catalogue's bounded code census and a different snapshot; keep stage/page/round and source distinct. Neither category numbers nor a listing alone establishes current vacancy or sanctioned intake. Curriculum and profile presence cannot prove an offering.

[TNEA Colleges directory](https://tneacolleges.com/colleges) is a secondary discovery and UX reference; unsupported attributes remain NEEDS_OFFICIAL_SOURCE. On inspection it displayed 435 active colleges and district, branch, type and autonomy controls; this is the site's own changing count, not an official 2026 count. No comprehensive field-by-field conflict audit has been completed. Do not use a directory-only result as governed offering evidence.

For each conflict retain `COLLEGE_CODE`, `PROGRAMME_CODE_IF_RELEVANT`, `FIELD`, `SEAT_MATRIX_VALUE`, `SEAT_MATRIX_SCOPE`, `SEAT_MATRIX_REFERENCE`, `COLLEGE_BOOKLET_VALUE`, `COLLEGE_BOOKLET_SCOPE`, `COLLEGE_BOOKLET_REFERENCE`, `SECONDARY_VALUE`, `SECONDARY_REFERENCE`, and `ACTION = USE_APPLICABLE_OFFICIAL_SOURCE / NEEDS_RECONCILIATION / NEEDS_OWNER_REVIEW / UNKNOWN`. Each reference retains source date/year, stream or stage where applicable and page/location. Preserve missing values as UNKNOWN or NOT REPORTED; do not silently merge conflicts. When official sources disagree, default to NEEDS_RECONCILIATION unless field responsibility and applicable date/scope clearly justify a value. A different snapshot or abbreviation alone is not automatically a conflict. No comprehensive conflict audit was completed; conflicts remain unassessed, not absent.

## Student flows and presentation contract

**Branch-first:** Existing approved educational profile → short reviewed explanation → exact code-linked source-year college/programme options → governed college context → optional compare → Things Worth Checking. Pending/held profile: no draft text or inferred mapping; explain pending status with a next action to browse verified exact programme names or other approved profiles. No marks requested.

**College-first:** Source-verified college → exact source-year programme list → reviewed linked existing profiles, with unmatched/held codes kept visible as unlinked where safe → optional compare → Things Worth Checking. A missing data field or source link displays UNKNOWN/NOT REPORTED rather than a guessed value. Keep source coverage and snapshot date visible. Never display “no colleges offer this” when the dataset is partial.

**Result:** Display official college name/code, exact programme name/code, approved profile name if linked, district/location, management type and autonomy as separate fields when verified; otherwise show unknown. Explain the relationship in student language: “This college appears because the official 2026 programme data lists this college and programme for the source stage shown.” Show source year, stage, direct source/page and explanation of limitations; distinguish verified versus recorded page locations. Do not imply any live offering. Existing approved bilingual wording is reusable only within its original scope; new wording requires human Tamil/English review.

**Filters:** Only student-selected, governed family/profile, exact programme code/name, district, management type, autonomy or college name. Missing metadata remains visible as unknown when no filter is selected; when a filter is applied explicitly describe excluded unknowns and permit reset. Default ordering is neutral and disclosed, not opaque recommendation. No Best, Top, Safe, Target, Dream, admission chance or quality score.

**Compare 2–3 explicitly selected options:** Side-by-side governed identity, exact programme/variant, source year, district, type and separate autonomy status with unknowns and sources. No winner, score or implied quality. Offer neutral reflection prompts such as “Which programme would you like to learn more about?” and “Which location should you check with your family?” Preserve each student's chosen options. Provide a simple back/continue action.

**Things Worth Checking:** Visibly separate known governed facts from questions about fees, hostel, transport, accreditation, current intake, scholarships/concessions and current counselling participation. These are prompts to consult current official/college sources, not facts or entitlement claims. Placement and career claims need separate evidence work and are not inferred. If no approved prompt or item exists, use an honest empty state and preserve a next action.
